## What this is
The verse text of the Geneva Bible as printed in 1599 (the edition most reprinted; the 1560 first edition's text is nearly identical and its marginal notes were revised in 1599). Psalm superscriptions are part of verse 1, as the Geneva printed them. Section headings in the source (0) are dropped: they are not the reformers' arguments.

## What this is NOT
The marginal notes, chapter arguments and cross-references are NOT in this source and are NOT served. The `geneva-notes` annotation source stays `declared` until a verified transcription is ingested.
